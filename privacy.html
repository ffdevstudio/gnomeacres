Gnome Acres privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Gnome Acres collects no personal or device data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

This policy covers the game Gnome Acres, made by FF Dev Studio ("we"), on every platform where it is sold.

Your farm stays on your device

Your farms, saves and settings are stored only on your own device. We never receive them. If your phone or computer makes its own backups (for example Android's device backup), your saves may be included in them; those backups belong to your account with that service, and we cannot see them.

Buying the game

The store you buy the game from (such as Google Play) handles the payment. We never see your payment details; stores only give developers sales reports without your personal information.

Crash reports from the store

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may show us anonymous crash and freeze reports for the game (device model, system version and where the game stopped). They contain nothing that identifies you, and we use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this sharing off in your device settings.

Children

Gnome Acres is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Internet access

The game plays offline. It does not open network connections of its own, except when you choose to follow a link (such as this page), which opens in your browser. On Android it does not even ask for the internet permission.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add something that uses your data, such as online features, we will update this page before that version is released and tell you about it in the game.
